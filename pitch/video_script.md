# 1-minute video: script and shot list

Recorded from the real app on the hero site in [demo_seeds.json](demo_seeds.json) (861 Park Avenue unless the team picks another). Screen recording at 1920×1080, dark theme, no cursor jitter: script the clicks. Voiceover or captions, about 130 words.

| # | Time | Shot | Voiceover / caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00 to 0:06 | The citywide map at night, calm clusters fading in. | "This is every public record that matters for building in New York, on one map." |
| 2 | 0:06 to 0:12 | Open the layer panel: sources with counts, dates, and one marked "Couldn't load". | "Each source has its date and link. If one fails to load, it says so. It never says zero." |
| 3 | 0:12 to 0:20 | Type the address; cinematic fly-to; lot facts appear during the flight. | "Pick a site. ForeSite finds its lot and every lot that touches it." |
| 4 | 0:20 to 0:28 | Close-up: the site and its neighbors outlined; the shared wall glows. | "Older attached neighbors, and the wall the project shares with them." |
| 5 | 0:28 to 0:36 | Site panel: the top 3 signals, for example the excavation-undermining reports, each with its date and source. | "What the record already shows, dated, with the source one click away." |
| 6 | 0:36 to 0:44 | Zoom out: similar cases arc in from across the city; hover one: "matches on" and "differs on". | "Similar cases from across the city: what matched, what differed, and the cause only as publicly reported." |
| 7 | 0:44 to 0:52 | Actions grouped by discipline; open one: owner, proof needed. | "Actions for the architect, the structural engineer, geotech and the contractor. Proof comes from the team." |
| 8 | 0:52 to 1:00 | Ask the chat a question; the answer's source chip flies the map; then the limitations panel. | "Ask in plain English. ForeSite isn't exhaustive or engineering advice. It's the shared starting point." |

**End card (2 s, can overlap shot 8):** "ForeSite. Before work starts."

### Rules for the edit
- Do not show Nov 11 or 1321 Flatbush Avenue.
- Text on screen stays up long enough to read (about 0.3 s per word).
- No owner, applicant or contractor names on screen, including in a record's raw text. Crop or blur if one appears.
- Keep the counts on screen exactly as the app shows them on recording day.
