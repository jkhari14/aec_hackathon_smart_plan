# ForeSite: 5-minute pitch

**Format** (from the third team meeting): about 1 minute of slides, 1 minute of video, 1 to 2 minutes of live demo, then a short wrap-up by persona. Judges: AEC Tech NYC 2026.

**Tone:** calm and factual. Never open with Nov 11. No blame, no "would have prevented", no forecast for any building.

## Slide 1: The problem (15 s)
**On screen:** "Building failures are rarely one mistake. They happen when several people each miss something."
**Say:** On an NYC job, the architect, the engineers, the contractor and DOB each see part of the picture. The warnings are often public: complaints from neighbors, violations, filings. But they sit in a dozen city datasets nobody reads together.

## Slide 2: What's already public (15 s)
**On screen:** the dataset names as a quiet grid: DOB complaints, violations, ECB, filings, 311, construction incidents, vacate orders, facade filings, flood maps, E-designations.
**Say:** It's all open data. What's missing is one view of a site and its neighbors, at the moment a decision is being made.

## Slide 3: ForeSite (15 s)
**On screen:** "ForeSite: a 3D map of NYC that shows the public record for a site and its neighbors, before work starts." A screenshot of the map home.
**Say:** For the architect who coordinates the project and the structural engineer. Pick any site and you see its neighbors, similar cases from across the city, early-warning patterns in the record, and actions by discipline.

## Slide 4: How it stays honest (15 s)
**On screen:** three chips: "Every fact shows its source and date." "Proof comes from the team, never from public data." "Not exhaustive, and not engineering advice."
**Say:** Every item links to where it came from. A failed query says it couldn't load; it never says zero. Past cases carry a cause only as publicly reported.

## Slide 5: Next step: forensics (15 s)
**On screen:** "After an event: index the documents, build a dated evidence ledger, link what matters."
**Say:** A forensic engineer we spoke with today said the hard part of their work is assembling thousands of pages into a story. The same dated records, weather, soil and case library are the start of that ledger. The user confirms every link.

## Slide 6: Close (15 s)
**On screen:** "ForeSite. One map. Every party. Before work starts." The repo link and "Built on NYC Open Data. Open source."
**Say:** Everyone on the project sees the same site, and each person sees what is theirs to check.

### Checks before presenting
- Every number on a slide comes from a source you can open. Re-run the queries the morning of the pitch.
- Say "similar cases", "patterns compared with similar sites", "signals". Never "predicts", "will fail", or "safe".
- If the live demo fails, play the video's demo section again and talk over it (see the fallback in the runbook).
