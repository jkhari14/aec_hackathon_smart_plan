# Persona wrap-up (45 seconds)

Two short first-person takes, spoken by the teammates who do these jobs. Keep each to about 20 seconds.

## The architect (applicant of record)
"I coordinate the project and I file it with DOB. Before ForeSite, finding out what was going on next door meant a dozen city websites and a lot of luck. Now the site, its neighbors and what each consultant needs to check are in one place my whole team can see. I can assign an item to the structural engineer and see the proof come back."

## The structural engineer
"I'm responsible for my design, and no tool replaces that. What this gives me is the context I'd otherwise have to dig for: which neighbors are older and attached, what's been reported about them, and how similar jobs across the city went. It tells me where to look harder. It says clearly what it doesn't know."

## Closing line (Ethnie)
"Everyone saw one piece. ForeSite shows the whole picture, and who owns each check."
